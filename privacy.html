Privacy Policy

Part Two Please · Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

Part Two Please is a storytelling channel run by one person. This policy covers "Part Two Please", the private tool we use to upload videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos to, and read basic information about, the owner's own YouTube channel (channel name, video titles, upload status and public statistics). It uses this only to upload our own videos and check that they published correctly.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or sell personal data from viewers or anyone else.
  • We don't access any YouTube or Google account other than the channel owner's.
  • We don't use Google user data for advertising, and we don't share it with third parties, except the service providers needed to perform the upload. Sign-in tokens are held by our integration provider (Composio) so the tool can act on the owner's behalf.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. When access is revoked, the tool can no longer upload or read anything.

Data retention

The tool keeps no copies of Google user data beyond what is needed to complete an upload. Our own video files and captions stay on our own computer.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: thedhamansh@gmail.com.
