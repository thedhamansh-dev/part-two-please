What this is

Part Two Please is an animated storytelling channel. We publish original, fictional short stories (drama, mystery, revenge and family chaos) as short videos on Instagram and TikTok, with full-length episodes on YouTube.

How it's made

Every story is original fiction written for this channel. Videos are produced with AI-assisted narration and animation and are labeled as AI-generated where the platform asks. No story describes real people or real events.

Our upload tool

We use a private, internal tool to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner, it never accesses anyone else's account, and it does not collect data from viewers. See the privacy policy.
